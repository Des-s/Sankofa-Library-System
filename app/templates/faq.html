Frequently Asked Questions

New here? Create an account to get started.

How do I register for a library account?

Click "Register" and sign up with your student ID and school email address. Your account will be created with pending status until library staff review it.

How long does approval take?

Most accounts are reviewed within one to two business days. You'll be able to log in as soon as a librarian approves your registration.

How do I borrow a physical book?

Browse the catalog online and visit the library with your library card. A librarian will check the book out to you — each member may borrow up to 3 books at a time for 14 days.

Can I read books online?

Yes — books marked "Digital Available" in the catalog can be read directly in your browser once you're logged in and your library card is verified. No downloads needed.

How are overdue fines calculated?

A daily rate applies for each day a physical book is returned after its due date. You can check your current balance from your dashboard at any time, and librarians can waive fines for exceptional circumstances.

How do I reset my password?

Use "Forgot password?" on the login page to have a temporary password emailed to you, or change it any time from your Profile once logged in.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions? We have answers.

How do I register for a library account?

Click "Get started" on the homepage and fill in your details using your @st.knust.edu.gh email. A librarian will review and approve your account, usually within one business day. Your library card number is issued immediately upon registration.

How many books can I borrow at once?

Students may borrow up to 5 physical books at a time for a 14-day loan period. Renewals can be requested if no other student has reserved the same title.

What happens if I return a book late?

Overdue fines are calculated at GHS 1.00 per day. You can view your current fine balance anytime from your dashboard. Librarians can apply waivers for exceptional circumstances.

Can I read digital books online?

Yes — books marked as "Digital Available" can be read online through our digital reader. You'll need to verify your library card number to start a reading session.

How do I get a library card?

Your library card is issued automatically when you register. The card number appears on your dashboard once your account is approved. Physical cards can be collected from the circulation desk.

What if I lose a book?

Please report lost books immediately to the library staff. A replacement fee will be assessed based on the book's value. Reports can be filed from the librarian dashboard under "Reports".

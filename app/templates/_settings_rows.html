FAQ
How do I borrow a physical book?

Visit the library with your library card; a librarian will check the book out to you.

How are fines calculated?

A daily rate applies for each day a physical book is returned after its due date. The rate is set by the library.

Can I read books online?

Yes — books marked "Digital" in the catalog can be read after verifying your library card number.

How do I reset my password?

Use "Forgot password?" on the login page, or change it any time from your Profile once logged in.
